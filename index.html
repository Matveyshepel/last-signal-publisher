Last Signal Publisher

Last Signal Publisher is a small publishing tool for Last Signal Stories, an English-language channel of original animated survival stories.

The tool lets the channel's creator sign in with their own TikTok account and post the channel's finished videos, with their titles and captions, to that account.

The TikTok connection is in preparation and will be used only after TikTok approves the app.

How it works

The creator signs in through TikTok's official authorization page and approves access. The creator chooses a finished video and reviews its details. The tool then sends the video to TikTok through the official TikTok API.
