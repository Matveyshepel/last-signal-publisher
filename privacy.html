Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Last Signal Publisher (the "App") handles information.

Who uses the App

The App is run by the creator of Last Signal Stories for their own channel. It has no sign-up for the public.

Information handled

The TikTok connection is in preparation. Once TikTok approves the App, it will handle only the following:

  • TikTok authorization tokens, issued after you approve access, used to act on your behalf.
  • Basic TikTok account information, only if you approve it, such as your display name and account identifier, used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Videos, titles, captions and hashtags chosen for publishing, and the post status returned by TikTok.

The App does not ask for your TikTok password. Sign-in happens on TikTok's own page.

How it is used

This information is used only to connect the account and publish the selected videos. It is not sold and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored and shared

Tokens and publishing records are stored locally on the operator's own computer, outside any public code repository. Videos and their details are sent to TikTok so they can be posted. TikTok handles them under its own privacy policy. No other sharing takes place unless the law requires it.

This website

This site has no accounts, forms, analytics or advertising and sets no cookies of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical data such as visitor IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the connection is active. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok account settings. To ask for local records to be deleted, contact the address below.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
